1. What is vitalCHECK Enterprise Health Check?

vitalCHECK Enterprise Health Check is an assessment tool that allows you to measure the overall health of your business. By answering 25 questions across 5 key pillars, you get in around 6 minutes a maturity score, a personalized mini-report and your first action recommendations.

Why assess your business?

  • Identify strengths: Highlight what's working well
  • Detect weaknesses: Spot areas for improvement
  • Prioritize actions: Know where to focus your efforts
  • Measure progress: Track evolution over time
  • Make informed decisions: Base your choices on concrete data

The 5 pillars of business health

  1. Leadership & Strategy — Vision, objectives and business steering
  2. Finance — Financial management, cash flow and profitability
  3. Sales & Marketing — Commercial strategies and customer acquisition
  4. Operations — Operational efficiency, processes and tools
  5. People & Organization — Team management and skills development

2. How does the assessment work?

Scoring system

Each question offers 3 answer options:

Example question:

"Do you have written, quantified and dated business objectives?"

  • Yes — 4 points: Quantified, dated objectives shared with the team
  • Partially — 2 points: General objectives, not formally written down
  • No — 0 points: No formal objectives defined

Score calculation

  • Pillar score: (sum of pillar answers / 20) × 100 — i.e. 5 questions × 4 pts max = 20 pts → score out of 100
  • Overall score: Average of the 5 pillar scores (0–100)
  • Estimated duration: About 6 minutes

The 5 maturity levels

Critical (0–39) Significant risks — immediate action required
Vulnerable (40–59) Fragile foundations — several areas need priority attention
Stable (60–79) Solid foundations — targeted adjustments will help move up a gear
Growth-Ready (80–89) Well-structured business — ready to accelerate
High Performance (90–100) Excellent organizational maturity — keep up the great work!

4. Understanding your results

Overall score

Your overall score (0–100) reflects the maturity of your business. It is the average of your 5 pillar scores.

How to interpret your overall score:

  • 90–100 — High Performance: Excellent organizational maturity across all key pillars. Keep up the great work!
  • 80–89 — Growth-Ready: Well-structured business, ready to accelerate its development.
  • 60–79 — Stable: Solid foundations. A few targeted adjustments will help you move up a gear.
  • 40–59 — Vulnerable: Fragile foundations. Several areas require priority attention.
  • 0–39 — Critical: Significant risks threatening sustainability. Immediate action is needed.

Scores by pillar

Each pillar is assessed across 5 questions and scored out of 100, letting you pinpoint your strengths and priority improvement areas.

Leadership & Strategy

Assesses clarity of vision, goal-setting and the ability to steer the business.

  • Key points: Written objectives, SWOT analysis, dashboard, strategic plan
  • Impact: Clear direction and informed decision-making

Finance

Evaluates financial health, cash flow management and profitability.

  • Key points: Financial statements, cash flow, margins, budget forecasts
  • Impact: Stability and investment capacity

Sales & Marketing

Analyzes customer acquisition, retention and communication strategies.

  • Key points: Value proposition, acquisition channels, sales pipeline, conversion rate
  • Impact: Revenue growth and market positioning

Operations

Measures the efficiency of internal processes and quality of execution.

  • Key points: Documented processes, management tools, KPIs, quality
  • Impact: Productivity, cost reduction and customer satisfaction

People & Organization

Evaluates team management, skills development and company culture.

  • Key points: Recruitment, training, motivation, roles and responsibilities
  • Impact: Collective performance and talent retention

6. Tips and recommendations

How to answer questions well

Our tips for a successful assessment:

  • Be honest: The goal is to identify real needs
  • Think about your current situation: Not about your future projects
  • Consult your team: Ask for your collaborators' opinions
  • Take your time: Think about each answer
  • Note your ideas: Use a notepad for your reflections

What to do after the assessment

Recommended action plan:

  1. Analyze your results: Identify the weakest pillars
  2. Prioritize actions: Start with critical areas
  3. Involve your team: Share results and objectives
  4. Plan improvements: Define concrete steps
  5. Measure progress: Retake the assessment in 6 months

7. Frequently asked questions

The diagnostic takes about 6 minutes and is designed to be completed in one session. Once submitted, your results are accessible at any time via the link sent by email.

The complete assessment takes about 6 minutes. 25 questions with 3 answer options each: Yes, Partially, No.

No, once the assessment is submitted, the answers are final. However, you can create a new assessment at any time.

We recommend retaking the assessment every 6 months to track your progress and adjust your improvement priorities.

8. Multi-Team Management (Premium Account)

The Multi-Team feature is available for Premium accounts. It allows a business owner to invite collaborators to vitalCHECK, creating a shared space where all the company's diagnostics are centralized.

Benefits of the team feature:

  • Centralized view: All company diagnostics gathered in one place
  • Simplified collaboration: Collaborators join the team in one click via an email invitation
  • Real-time tracking: Completed diagnostics appear automatically in the team view
  • Data security: Each team only sees its own diagnostics — no data from other companies is accessible

How to invite a team member

1Go to the "My Team" page

From your dashboard, click on My Team in the navigation menu.

2Send an invitation

Click "Invite a member", enter the collaborator's email address and confirm. An invitation email is automatically sent with a link valid for 7 days.

Important: If your collaborator cannot find the invitation email in their inbox, they must check their Spam / Junk Mail folder. Invitation emails may be automatically filtered by some email clients (Gmail, Outlook, etc.).

3Accept the invitation

The collaborator clicks the link in the email. They are redirected to a page where they can:

  • Log in if they already have a vitalCHECK account
  • Create an account if they don't have one yet

Once authenticated, they automatically join the team and access the My Team page.

4View shared diagnostics

On the My Team page, all members can see the history of completed company diagnostics, with detailed scores and results.

Key limits to know:

  • Maximum 5 members per team (owner included)
  • Invitations are valid for 7 days — after expiry, send a new invitation
  • An invitation link can only be used once

The invitation link shows "Invalid invitation"?

This can happen if the link has expired (more than 7 days) or has already been used. In this case, the team owner must send a new invitation from the My Team page.
